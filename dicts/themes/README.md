# Thematic vocabulary packs

The English and Russian lists in this directory are small, manually curated MultiSwitcher additions for software, design, product names, and informal language. They are not copied from CSpell, FrequencyWords, OpenCorpora, or RuLexNorm. The app bundles them under the dedicated MIT license in `LICENSE`; `manifest.json` records each pack's version, count, byte size, and SHA-256.

The lists only mark spellings as acceptable. They do not normalize slang or force a replacement. The software-terms, fonts, and en-slang packages in [CSpell dictionaries](https://github.com/streetsidesoftware/cspell-dicts/tree/main/dictionaries) are useful terminology references; their entries are not bundled here. [RuLexNorm](https://github.com/SmartEngines/RuLexNorm) is useful for test examples, not a source of automatic replacement rules.

Review every addition against the layout-conversion regression corpus before enabling it by default. User rules and personal exceptions keep precedence over these general vocabularies.
